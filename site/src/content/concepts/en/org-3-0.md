## Definition

**Organization 3.0** — a concept of an agent-centric company in which strategic decisions are automatically translated into goals, and goals become prompts for AI agents that form an operational plan.


## Evolution of Organizations

| Version | Management | Coordination |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| Org 1.0 | Hierarchy + orders | Personal communication |
| Org 2.0 | Hierarchy + SaaS systems (CRM, ERP) | Electronic tracking systems |
| Org 3.0 | Decisions → Goals → Agents | AI agents as the operational layer |

Traditional organizations are designed around **the constraints of human communication**. A general cannot give a direct order to each of hundreds of thousands of soldiers—hence the need for a hierarchy. AI agents remove this constraint.

## Key Shift

Today: Strategy → Managers → Tasks → Employees (5–7 layers, enormous losses in information transmission)

Org 3.0: Strategy → Goals → Prompts → Agents → Outcome (minimal friction)

## Current State

As of 2026-05: the concept is at the experimental stage. Examples of partial implementation:
- Venture funds using agentic systems to source and analyze founders
- Shopify, Block — AI-first processes, including a requirement for AI justification before hiring

## Operationalization Through Block

The article From Hierarchy to Intelligence makes Org 3.0 more operational. Block describes not merely agents in place of managers, but four layers of the company:

1. **Capabilities** - atomic financial building blocks.
2. **World model** - a model of the company and its customers.
3. **Intelligence layer** - the layer that composes capabilities into decisions.
4. **Interfaces** - products and devices as delivery surfaces.

Key clarification: an AI-native organization requires a world model of the company; otherwise, agents remain an optimization of the existing hierarchy rather than a replacement for its information function.

## Preconditions for Adoption

1. A personal AI OS for every leader
2. A team AI OS as shared infrastructure
3. Only then—the corporate level
