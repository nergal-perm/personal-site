# Slice S20 — Late-bound target activation

## Signal

Requirements SEM-04, SEM-05, REL-02 (semantic projection), REL-03 (activation provenance) are specified in openspec/specs/ but not yet implemented. Without late-bound activation, semantic occurrence references (landed in S19) can only ever render as labels or ever require reapproving the referrer — there's no way for a link to "come alive" when its target is later approved, defeating the purpose of the stable occurrence map.

## Problem Type

synthesis

## Problem Profile

## P2W Readiness

p2w_blocked

## Why Now

S19 just closed (prob-20260817-035bb310); S20 is the next slice in the governing implementation-plan.md sequence and completes Milestone D (semantic publication frontier) together with S18-S19.

## Scope

exporter-java release-materialization path (semantic projection at release time); no changes to prepare/candidate/approval workflow, no migration/apply logic.

## Acceptance

A two-publication in-memory acceptance test covers the complete state sequence (target private -> label; target approved -> localized link; target unpublished -> label again) and asserts the referrer's approved snapshot hash is unchanged throughout. Requirements SEM-04, SEM-05, REL-02 (semantic projection), REL-03 (activation provenance) are satisfied. Migration and automatic rewriting of any approved referrer remain explicitly excluded per the implementation plan.

## Resolution

Addressed by the archived S20 change. The exporter now defers ordinary-link activation to release time, preserves approved referrer bytes while targets are approved or unpublished, routes from the target's own publication kind, and records release-output provenance. The completed acceptance sequence and final whole-branch review were followed by a fresh green Maven suite.
