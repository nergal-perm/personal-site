# S17c — bibliography/book kind and library-route evidence (ADM-03, ADM-04, PCM-02, PCM-06)

## Signal

PublicationKinds.installed() currently registers only blog/essay, blog/note, and blog/claim, so NoteIntake, InspectPublicationHandler, and write-publication-contract still reject or omit bibliography/book even though the live site already exposes bibliography entries at /{language}/library/{id}/. site/src/content.config.ts requires bibliography entries to carry at least one author and permits additional book-specific metadata (publication, publicationDate, start, end, readingStatus, use, boundary, selectedQuote), while BookPage.astro renders authors, publication, readingStatus, selectedQuote, use, and boundary from the managed frontmatter. After S17b the exporter can carry ordered translatable PublicFields plus one opaque structuredData fragment, but no PublicationKind yet owns bibliography/book admission, /library/ route policy, contract output, or the book-specific policy for which optional metadata is translated versus carried unchanged.

## Constraints

- No generic schema/reflection framework and no new collection/content-type switches in generic orchestration.
- Reuse the existing PublicationKind/PublicationKinds seam and the claim-era ordered PublicField plus structuredData carriers unless a narrowly scoped book requirement proves another policy is necessary.
- The existing blog/essay, blog/note, and blog/claim prepare-approve-release path and contract output must remain green and observably unchanged.
- The new kind must own its bibliography/book identity and /library/ route policy, and write-publication-contract must enumerate it deterministically alongside existing kinds.
- The slice stays within the existing process boundaries: no new production boundary adapter, and the in-memory acceptance subset remains under one second.

## Optimization Targets

- One bibliography/book fixture completes admit to prepare to approve to release through the same handlers already proven for blog kinds and installs site content under /ru/library/ and /en/library/.
- Adding bibliography/book requires one explicit PublicationKinds registration plus book-specific admission/projection code, without edits to existing kinds or new generic orchestration branches.
- The generated publication contract describes bibliography/book with its required authors field and the chosen policy for optional book metadata.

## Acceptance

One selected bibliography/book note with valid identity and at least one author is admitted, prepared into RU and EN candidate content, approved, and installed into the managed site under the library route through the existing handlers and CLI flow; write-publication-contract emits a deterministic bibliography/book entry; the existing blog-kind acceptance suites remain green.

## Blast Radius
