# S17b — blog/claim kind and whole-publication-value evidence (ADM-03, ADM-04, PCM-02, PCM-06)

## Signal

EssayPublicationKind and NotePublicationKind (publication-exporter/src/main/java/dev/eugene/publicationexporter/admission/) are today structurally identical: same FIELD_RULES (publicId/id/title/description), no structured body beyond raw markdown, and AdmittedPublication (admission/AdmittedPublication.java) only carries (kind, identity, sourceId, title, description) with no room for extra required fields. site/src/content.config.ts already defines a third real blog content type, blogClaim (contentType:'claim'), sharing blogShared with essay/note but additionally requiring a non-optional `statement` field plus five relationship arrays (supports/opposes/assumes/refines/contradicts, each `{label, target?}`) and a `sources` array of `{link?, attestation?, evidence?, locator?, confidence?}` — a genuinely different, richer required-field shape the exporter cannot yet admit, prepare, approve, or release.

## Problem Profile

thin

## P2W Readiness

p2w_candidate

## Why Now

openspec/implementation-plan.md sequences S17b immediately after S17a (closed, prob-20260811-ab6a410a) as the second rung of the S17a-S17f content-kind ladder (Milestone C). Its own blocking prerequisite, prob-20260811-0b8b9f2d, is now resolved (dec-20260811-02b96a37), clearing the path.

## Scope

publication-pipeline

## Constraints

- At most one new production boundary adapter — none is expected; claim admission/preparation/release is in-process dispatch over the existing kind-selected prepare/approve/release path
- No generic schema/reflection framework; ClaimPublicationKind implements PublicationKind directly, selected via the existing PublicationKinds registry
- Only claim's genuinely new required-field shape (statement, relationship arrays, sources) may drive a whole-publication-value refactor of localized-content/translation/snapshot/hash/diff carriers; do not generalize fields or body rules beyond what the claim fixture requires
- If a whole-publication-value refactor is triggered, migrate every dependent carrier as one coherent behaviour-preserving change — no dual essay/generic paths left behind
- The existing blog/essay and blog/note paths (all prior slice acceptance tests) must keep passing unchanged
- In-memory acceptance subset stays well under the 1s slice budget
- Must not reintroduce prob-20260811-0b8b9f2d's cross-kind (collection, publicId) collision for the new claim kind — the same collection is now shared by three kinds

## Optimization Targets

- blog/claim completes the same prepare→approve→release path already proven for blog/essay and blog/note, exercised through one new acceptance fixture, including its required statement field and relationship arrays surviving translation/candidate/approved/release/site projection
- Adding blog/claim changes only its own kind object/fixtures, one explicit PublicationKinds.installed() registration line, and genuinely new site-facing projection code — no new collection/type conditionals appear in kind-neutral orchestration
- write-publication-contract's output includes a complete blog/claim entry (statement required, relationship/source arrays described) alongside the unchanged blog/essay and blog/note entries

## Acceptance

One blog/claim fixture note (contentType 'claim', all shared blogShared fields, a non-blank statement, and at least one populated relationship array) is admitted, prepared into an RU+EN candidate pair, approved as an approved snapshot, and released into site output through the same handlers/CLI commands already proven for blog/essay and blog/note — with no change to blog/essay's or blog/note's observable behaviour (their full existing acceptance suites stay green). write-publication-contract emits a complete, independently correct blog/claim entry. The cross-kind collision guard closed by dec-20260811-02b96a37 continues to hold for a three-kind blog collection.

## Blast Radius

publication-exporter admission package (new ClaimPublicationKind; possibly AdmittedPublication or its successor if a whole-publication-value refactor is triggered), contract package (ClaimPublicationKind's contract; PublicationKinds.installed() registration), prepare/candidate/site frontmatter projection for claim-specific fields (statement, relationship arrays, sources), new acceptance/unit fixtures for blog/claim. Depending on the whole-publication-value decision, may also touch translation, candidate/approved snapshot, hash, and diff carriers shared with essay/note.
